Profile

I build systems that production floors depend on.

Seven years across manufacturing, semiconductor and government work has taught me that most software problems start as process problems. So I spend as much time on the floor with engineers and supervisors as I do in the editor, gathering requirements and turning them into workflows people actually use.

That's meant configuring in-house MES/ERP systems and Electronic Data Capture, wiring invoicing straight into on-premise SAP Business One, running full UAT and FAT deployment cycles, and shipping Laravel/Vue platforms and Flutter apps on top. I work efficiently with minimal supervision, and just as comfortably inside a team.
