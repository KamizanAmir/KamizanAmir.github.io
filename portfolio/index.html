SYSTEMS ARENA / FIELD GUIDE

Ready, player one.

Defeat all bugs in three waves. Move near an enemy and pulse to deal damage. A bug touching you reduces integrity; reach zero and the run ends.

  • Move: WASD / arrows, or the on-screen direction pad.
  • API Pulse: Space or PULSE. Area attack; 0.6 second cooldown.
  • Flutter Dash: Shift or DASH. Evade in your movement direction; 3 second cooldown.
  • DevOps Shield: E or SHIELD. Block damage for 2 seconds; 7 second cooldown.
  • Pause: P, Escape, or the pause button. Switching tabs also pauses.
