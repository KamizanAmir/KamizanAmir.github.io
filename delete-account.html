Delete your KamiTrack account

KamiTrack — developed by Kamizan Amirudin · Last updated: 8 August 2026

You can delete your KamiTrack account and everything stored against it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

1. From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open KamiTrack and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap More settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is removed immediately and you are signed out. Nothing is kept in a recycle bin and there is no undo.

2. By email (if you have already uninstalled)

Email kamizan980505@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my KamiTrack account”.

We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from the same email address, so that nobody can delete an account that is not theirs.

What is deleted

Deleted immediatelyWhy
Your profile: name, username, photo, cover image, biography, showcase artwork, avatar frame It is yours.
Step counts, distance, active hours, water, and every recorded run including its route Activity data belongs to the account that produced it.
Tasks and reminders Private to you already.
Posts, comments, likes, reshares and uploaded images Removed from other people's feeds along with the account.
Direct and group messages you sent, and your group memberships Removed with the account.
Friends, followers, following, achievements and sign-in history Removed with the account.
Your login credentials and push notification token The account no longer exists, so neither should the way into it.
KeptWhy, and for how long
Reports you filed about other people's content — with your name removed A report is evidence about the account being reported, not about you. If deleting your own account erased the reports you had filed, reporting would stop being worth anything. Your identity is severed from them immediately; the report itself is kept while the matter is open.
Groups you created that still have other members in them Ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member. Your messages and membership are still deleted — the group survives so that everyone else does not lose their conversation.
There is no retention period on the rest. Everything in the first table is removed when the deletion runs, not queued for later clean-up. Routine encrypted database backups may hold copies for up to 30 days before they age out; these are never used to restore a deleted account.
