Last updated: 26 August 2026

2. Location, specifically

KamiTrack records location only during a run you started by tapping "Start Run", and only for as long as that run is active. While it is, Android shows a permanent notification so you always know recording is happening. Stopping the run stops the recording.

The app does not request background location access. It does not track you when no run is active, and it never records your location silently.

If you opt in to the neighbourhood leaderboard, your location is rounded to an approximate area of roughly one kilometre before it is stored. Your exact coordinates are never shared with other users, and the feature is off unless you turn it on.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your health, fitness or location data for advertising.
  • We do not show your exact location to any other user.

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication and stored files.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging) — delivers push notifications.
  • Google Play Services / Google Maps — sign-in and map rendering.
  • Jamendo — supplies the music catalogue you pick a profile theme from.
  • Radio Browser — supplies the station list for radio during runs and workouts.
  • Google Gemini API — processes food photos and Helper messages, for that request only.
  • Google Play Billing — processes payment for VIP subscriptions and credit purchases.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads and the optional "watch an ad for credits" reward.

Jamendo and Radio Browser receive your device's IP address when the app requests music or a station list, as any web request does. Neither is sent your account, your name, or anything you have recorded in the app.

5. How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, activity history, runs, tasks and messages. Push tokens are automatically removed after 180 days of inactivity.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Profile → More settings → Delete account. You are asked to type DELETE to confirm, so it cannot happen by accident, and then it is done — there is no waiting period and no need to contact anyone.

If you cannot reach the app — you have uninstalled it, or cannot sign in — email kamizan980505@gmail.com from the address on your account and the request is actioned within 30 days. What is removed, and the two narrow things that are kept, are listed in full on the account deletion page.

7. Purchases and refunds

VIP subscriptions and credit packs are billed entirely by Google Play — KamiTrack never sees or stores your payment card details.

Refunds are handled by Google under Google Play's own refund policy, including its standalone right to a refund within 48 hours of purchase, no reason required, requested directly through Google Play (Play Store → Account → Order history). Outside that window, refund eligibility is at Google's discretion. If a refund is granted, credits already spent are not reversed or re-granted, since they were already used for what they paid for. We cannot issue refunds ourselves for purchases made through Play Billing.

8. Children

KamiTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Email kamizan980505@gmail.com to exercise any of these.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will show a notice before the change takes effect. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
