KamiTrack — FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH

com.kamitrack.app · Kamizan Amirudin

What the video shows

  1. The user opens Profile → Settings and finds Background step tracking, which is off by default.
  2. The user turns the switch on. Android's Physical activity permission is requested and granted.
  3. An ongoing notification appears — “KamiTrack · Step tracking” — and stays visible for as long as the service runs, so the work is noticeable to the user while they are not in the app.
  4. The user leaves KamiTrack entirely and walks with the phone.
  5. Returning to the app, today's step total has advanced — the steps taken outside the app were counted.
  6. The user turns the switch back off. The service stops and the notification disappears.

Why the permission is needed

KamiTrack is a daily step counter. The step total it shows, the goals it measures against and the streaks it awards are only honest if the count keeps up while the phone is in a pocket — which is when almost all walking happens. Without a foreground health service the count stops the moment Android reclaims the process, and the user is shown a number far below what they actually walked.

The service reads the device's hardware step counter (TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) and nothing else. It has no access to, and never requests, heart rate, body sensors or any other health data.

It is entirely opt-in. The switch is off until the user turns it on, it is never enabled on the user's behalf, an ongoing notification is shown the whole time it runs, and that notification links back to the setting that turns it off. Step counts belong to the signed-in account and are never shared with anyone unless the user chooses to post them.
